More about your prompts

What Kept by you and Test score mean
Kept by you
Of what this prompt produced in real use, the share you kept. Each prompt says what kept means in its drawer.
Test score
A version replayed on your last 40 decisions: how often it agreed with what you decided, and the guardrail checks.
Rollback rules

A version that passes its test runs on a share of runs first, and rolls back if Kept by you falls too low.

A prompt can keep its own rules: open its Details, then This prompt’s rollback rules. Only what you change there differs from these.

How testing works
  1. 1You save a version. It’s a draft: nothing runs it yet.
  2. 2Your deputy tests it. It replays your last 40 decisions and runs the guardrail checks. That’s the Test score. It doesn’t pass if the Test score is below your minimum, or more than the points you allow below the Test score of the version in use; then it never runs, so nothing rolls back.
  3. 3It passes, then it’s tried. It runs on a share of runs for a few days while Kept by you is watched.
  4. 4It goes into use, or it rolls back. If Kept by you falls short of your rules in its trial, it rolls back and the version before is used again.

You’re told each time: a notice on the page you’re on, a line in your deputy’s Activity, and a To-do when a test doesn’t pass or a version rolls back.
