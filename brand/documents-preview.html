            How custom templates work

            Four steps to your own layout. Your deputy fills it for every new draft.

            1. 1

              Start from a copy

              Download a built-in template as Markdown. Change it in any text editor.

            2. 2

              Place the placeholders

              Put {{name}} where your name goes, and the others wherever you want them.

            3. 3

              Mind the page

              US Letter, 8.5 by 11 inches, with 0.75 inch margins. Your page setting above sets the length.

            4. 4

              Upload it

              Choose Upload a .md file above. Your deputy checks it first, in this browser.

            What your deputy does with it

            • Fills each placeholder from your profile and the text it tailored for the role.
            • Keeps your layout: your sections, their order, and your own words.
            • Counts the pages against your page setting and trims the draft to fit.
            • Flags a draft that still runs over in Materials, before you approve it.
            The placeholders and what fills each

              On a line of its own, a placeholder fills a block, such as your jobs or a paragraph; inside a line, it fills in place. One on its own line with nothing to fill is left out.

              What your deputy checks, and the limits

              Before it uses a template

              • It’s a Markdown file, .md or .markdown.
              • It isn’t empty, and holds at most 20,000 characters.
              • It has {{name}}.
              • It uses only the placeholders listed.

              If one fails, it says which and how to fix it, and keeps nothing.

              What it never changes

              • Your layout: your sections, their order, your headings, and your words.
              • Your type: Markdown holds no fonts, so your template prints in the Classic look.
              • The facts: it never adds a result, a job, or a skill you don’t have.
              • Your original résumé and CV in your library.

              Markdown only: Word, PDF, and images aren’t read. In this prototype a template stays in this browser.
