Charts · 09

Data colours

Used on every chart, key swatch, ring, bar, and fit or stage dot (round 5, D1). Data has its own colours, tuned to sit together on the paper, apart from the brand's buttons, links, and status colours, which do not change. Every fill keeps 3:1 or more on the card and on the track, and each chart's parts stay apart for people with protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia (the rows below simulate them).

--viz-green, --viz-blue, --viz-orange, --viz-ink, --viz-quiet (data-tone="green|blue|orange|ink|quiet") · --viz-teal, --viz-indigo, --viz-gold, --viz-wine (longer series) · --viz-used (the hatching), --viz-left (data-meter-kind="watch" indigo, "assist" teal), --viz-bonus, --viz-bought, --viz-track · span.viz-swatch[data-tone | data-part] · JobDeputyComponents.palette (the words for each colour)

The chart tones · the store's FITS and STAGES name them; contrast measured on this page

  • Green Strong fit, Interviewing and Offer, on your résumé, a done stage
  • Blue Good fit, Applied, a neutral series
  • Orange Approval, something that needs you
  • Charcoal Tailoring, emphasis
  • Grey Stretch, Shortlisted, not on your résumé yet

The meters' parts · in the order they are spent

  • Hatched grey Used: spent this period
  • Indigo Left, on Keep watch (the night)
  • Teal Left, on Deputy Assist (AI credits or your key)
  • Green Left, on a meter with no kind
  • Gold Bonus: earned from a friend’s first search
  • Wine Bought: added in the Shop

Longer series · a stacked chart's groups take these in order

  1. 1. Blue
  2. 2. Green
  3. 3. Orange
  4. 4. Wine
  5. 5. Grey
  6. 6. Charcoal
  7. 7. Indigo

The first five stay apart in every colour vision. Teal and gold stay with the meters, so a chart of AI work never borrows Deputy Assist's left or the bonus.

As people with colour-blindness see them · simulated (Machado, Oliveira, and Fernandes, 2009)

  • Normal vision
  • Protanopia
  • Deuteranopia
  • Tritanopia

In forced colours each tone takes one system colour (green Highlight, blue LinkText, grey GrayText, charcoal CanvasText, orange a dashed outline), the longer series an outline in a system colour, and the meter's parts four looks: used hatched, left Highlight, bonus LinkText, and bought CanvasText.
