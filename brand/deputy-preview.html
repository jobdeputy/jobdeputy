Deputy companion · cast sheet

Your deputy, beside every task.

One smiling panda teammate travels with you through every signed-in page. Its pose shows the work under way, the tool in its right paw names it, the duty line says it, and a short line of speech says what it is doing. It answers what you do, makes a small gesture now and then at rest, gives every tour, and stops when you ask.

Every card holds its final pose until you replay it, and Replay all plays them one after another. Pause motion is remembered in this browser; reduced motion shows every pose still.

01 · One hand rule

A tool in the right paw, always.

Your deputy smiles at every size. The right paw holds the tool of the moment in every state, moment, and reaction; the left paw is free to rest, wave, point, or cheer; and the bamboo leans on the ledge beside the panda, a little behind his left side, never in a paw. Where only the face shows (the rail medallion and the chip), the tool shows on a small badge at the face’s edge.

08 · Paused

Paused means nothing moves.

Pause motion stops every animation on the page, not only your deputy, and holds on every page until Play motion. The duty reads Motion paused beside a pause mark, and the button reads Play motion. These deputies are pinned paused; press Play motion on one to resume just that one, or use Pause motion above for the whole sheet.

14 · Motion rules

Calm by default.

  • One hand rule The right paw always holds the tool of the moment; the left paw is free; the bamboo leans beside the panda, never in a paw. Paws touch the face only to cover the eyes on the entry stage, while a password is typed.
  • Still poses Every state starts and ends still. Poses change with a 618ms settle, from one page to the next too: a new page starts on the last pose and settles into its own.
  • One-time motion Each state moves once, for 2.605s or less, after the settle.
  • Loops only while working Search, tailor, fill, and test repeat every 5.236s only while work is in progress. Pending turns its dot every 987ms.
  • A quiet blink, a small gesture One blink every 5.236 to 8.472s, and at rest one gesture every 13 to 21s that suits the work. On app pages the deputy never follows the pointer.
  • Reactions Each action gets one reaction: its motion ends within 1.6s, its pose and line stay 2.6s, then the deputy settles back without replaying the page’s motion.
  • Pause motion Stops every animation on the page, the deputy’s included, and every timer; it is remembered in this browser and holds on every page. The duty reads Motion paused beside a pause mark, and it says “Deputy motion paused.” once. Play motion restarts a live work loop, never finished motion.
  • Reduced motion No animation: poses swap instantly, the spinner becomes a still dot with “Working…”, and the session greeting is skipped.
