Getting started

How does JobDeputy work?

You add the career sites and job boards to search. Your deputy reads them, keeps the roles that match your profile, and ranks them in Job matches. You choose what moves to Applications, where your deputy tailors your résumé and cover letter for your review. At launch it can also fill and submit an application, only after you approve it.

Does JobDeputy apply for jobs without asking me?

No. Your deputy finds roles, drafts your materials, and reviews them, then waits for you to approve each one. At launch, once you approve, it can apply in the background within your rules, and you can watch any application as it fills. In this prototype nothing is ever sent to an employer.

Why do I sign an agreement, and what if a new version comes?

The User Agreement says what your deputy does for you and what you agree to. You sign it in one step when you create your account: one sentence, then Sign and continue, as the name on your account, with nothing to type. You sign each new version the same way, after reading what changed. We remind you in the app and by email for two weeks; if a new version still isn’t signed 14 days after it’s published, your deputy’s work pauses until you sign: searches, Keep watch checks, filling in applications, and drafting.

Signing in, seeing everything, exporting your data, deleting your account, Help, and signing never pause. We keep every copy you sign permanently, apart from your account, so you and we can always show what you agreed to. Settings, Account has each copy you signed and any version waiting for you.

How do I replay a tour or change my setup?

Choose Take the tour at the top of any page, or start any page's tour from Demos or from Show me around in the account menu or your deputy's panel; your deputy gives every tour. Run setup again, in Demos, asks the setup questions with your saved answers filled in.

Can I skip setup and finish it later?

Yes. Every setup step has Skip the rest, and the first has Skip setup for now. Your deputy adds each step you skipped to your To-do, and each item opens the right place: your résumé, a first source, your roles, or your AI setting. The Dashboard works with nothing answered.

What's in Settings, and where did things move?

Settings has seven tabs, each its own page: Account (how you sign in, the emails we send you, your home Region, the agreements you signed, your data, and deleting your account), Connections (Gmail and its daily scan, LinkedIn, your site accounts, and your AI keys), Prompts (every prompt, its versions and tests, and the one place to edit them), AI & credits (your AI setting, keys, and limits), Usage (your runs and tokens over any time, and how long they last), Purchases (your balances, one quick buy, and every receipt), and Activity (everything you, your deputy, and JobDeputy did). Profile keeps what your deputy uses to apply, and Your rules, how your deputy applies, are in Applications. Old links still open the right place.

What does the large account demo show?

How each page handles far more data than it can show, with a much bigger sample account. Charts show their top five plus Other, long lists show the first few with See all, and filters over many values become a list you can search. Start it with See a large account in Demos; Back to the usual sample returns your own sample, exactly as you left it.

Job matches & fit

What do Roles found, To review, In Applications, and Not for me mean?

27Roles found =equals 20To review +plus 5In Applications +plus 2Not for me

Roles found counts every role your searches kept. Each role is in exactly one place: To review in Job matches until you decide, in Applications once you add it, and Not for me once you set it aside, where you can bring it back. Job matches is a queue: adding a role or setting it aside takes it out and opens the next one. So the three always add up to Roles found.

What do the fit colours mean?
  • Strong fit 4 roles. A target role, with most of what it asks. Open in Job matches
  • Good fit 9 roles. A target role, with a gap to check. Open in Job matches
  • Stretch 14 roles. A level, place, or field beyond your choices. Open in Job matches

Your deputy compares each role's title and place with your target roles and preferences, and its keywords with your résumé; Why it fits on each role shows what matched and what's missing. The same names and colours appear on the Dashboard and in Job matches, where choosing a colour filters to it. Fit labels are a launch preview.

Why does Job matches show a page at a time?

So a long list stays quick to scan. Every long list shows where you are ("13 to 24 of 40"), Previous and Next above and below it, and a page size you choose: 12, 24, or 48. The page is kept in the address, and changing it brings the list's top into view, so you never scroll back up. Changing the view or a filter starts again on page 1, and a link to one role opens the page that holds it.

Applications & résumés

What do the application stages and colours mean?
  • Shortlisted 2 roles. You chose it; your deputy has not started the materials. Open in Applications
  • Tailoring 1 role. Your deputy is drafting the résumé and cover letter. Open in Applications
  • Approval 1 role. The drafts wait for your approval. Open in Applications
  • Applied 2 roles. Sent to the employer, by you or your deputy. Open in Applications
  • Interviewing 1 role. You have an interview in progress. Open in Applications
  • Offer 0 roles. You have an offer. Open in Applications

Applications is one board of the roles you chose, and each card moves along it as the work happens. Every card says who did each step: you, or your deputy when your rules let it approve or apply by itself. An email can move a card too, and every move can be undone. Closed roles (not selected, withdrawn, or declined) stay out of the active count: you have 1.

What does Approve materials mean?

It tells your deputy that a role's tailored résumé and cover letter are ready to use. Approve and apply, on the card, does both in one step: once approved, your deputy applies in the background within your rules, and Watch shows the form as it fills, if you want to see it. You can undo the approval until the role is marked applied. In this prototype nothing is sent.

What is Review, then auto-apply?

It's how your deputy applies unless you change it. Your deputy drafts your résumé and cover letter and reviews them itself, with a score and named checks such as the posting's must-haves and that every claim is in your profile. You approve each one in a single step, then your deputy applies in the background within your rules: a daily limit, quiet hours, only when no new answers are needed, and only with the agreement signed.

When a form asks something new, the role shows Needs you with the questions, and your answers fill in as the run continues. Auto-approve rules come set up but off. Change any rule, or Reset to the system default, in Your rules, at the top of Applications. Planned for launch, Phase 2: nothing is sent in this prototype.

What does the autofill preview submit, and when is the form read?

The preview lists the exact answers your deputy would submit; the employer's own page looks different. Your deputy reads the form's questions when you add a role to Applications, and again just before filling. Self-identification answers come from Profile, and you can answer differently for one application. Nothing is submitted in this prototype.

Which résumé template should I use, and how many pages?

Templates are Markdown: Classic (the default), Modern, and Compact, or upload your own .md template. We recommend a concise one-page résumé, and you can allow two. The editor's preview shows exactly the pages that print. Your setting is 1 page.

Which résumé does my deputy tailor from?

Your default résumé, or your default CV when a role asks for one, both chosen in Résumés: now Alex_Morgan_Resume.pdf and Engineering_Leadership_CV.docx. Each tailored draft starts from it, and you check every change before you approve.

Searches & Keep watch

What is a run?

A run is one search of one source: your deputy reads the page, checks each role against your profile, and keeps the ones that fit. A role found twice is counted once. Each source lists its runs on its card in Searches, with how many roles each one checked and kept.

Every search uses one run from your allowance, whoever starts it: you, with a new source or Run again, or Keep watch at night, one for each source it checks. Each Gmail scan uses one too, the daily scan and Scan now, and so does each daily summary email if you turn it on. AI work is counted in tokens.

Why does a source need attention?

Its last run stopped: the page may need a sign-in, block automated reading, take too long to load, or list no jobs. The source and its history stay, so you can check the address and choose Retry on its card in Searches. Right now 1 of your sources needs attention.

What is Keep watch?

An optional feature, planned for launch: your deputy reruns the sources you choose every night and adds any new roles to Job matches. It stays off until you set a source to Nightly with Keep watch in Searches. You get 10 free runs a month for every search, and Keep watchFree runs return every month, and Keep watch uses one run for each source it checks. Your next check is Sat, Oct 3, 02:00 Pacific, for Google Careers and Microsoft Careers. None of your sources has it on yet.

AI, keys & prompts

What is Deputy Assist?

Deputy Assist is your deputy's AI work: reading job listings, scoring fit, tailoring your résumé, writing cover letters, drafting application answers and replies, reading application emails, and checking readiness. It's counted in tokens: on your own key, or on AI credits on our platform. Each search, Keep watch check, Gmail scan, and daily email is counted in runs. You can see both over any time in Usage. You choose what runs Deputy Assist, or turn it off and filter roles by keywords instead.

What runs Deputy Assist, and can one search use a different AI?

One setting for your account runs every search, Keep watch check, and draft, with one model: your OpenAI or Anthropic key with the model you chose for it, AI credits on the platform model you choose, or keyword filtering, which turns Deputy Assist off. There's no separate setting for a source or a task. A single run differs only when it says so: Run again can use AI credits for that one run, and a key that reaches its limit can fall back to them. Each run keeps what it ran with in its history, and a change applies from the next run.

Can I use my own AI key, and change its model?

Yes. Add an OpenAI or Anthropic key in AI & credits, one per provider. Work on your key uses none of your AI credits and we never charge for it, though your provider may; the job data a run needs goes to that provider, which you agree to when you save the key. On a saved key, Change model lists the model we recommend first. Larger models cost more per token on your key and can use more tokens.

Can I limit the tokens my own key uses?

Yes. In AI & credits, set an amount of tokens for a day, a week, or a month, counted in UTC, and choose what happens when your key reaches it: Pause AI work until the limit resets, or Use platform credits, so Deputy Assist switches to your AI credits while at least a search's worth is left. A run already going finishes, and its tokens still count. Only your own keys count toward it.

Your limit is 250,000 tokens a month, with 49,810 used this month; it resets Sun, Nov 1, 00:00 UTC. You haven't set a limit, so your key runs without one. When it's reached, AI work pauses.

Can I see and change the prompts your deputy uses?

Yes. Each of the 8 AI tasks has its prompt in Settings, Prompts: reading listings, scoring fit, tailoring the résumé, writing the cover letter, drafting application answers, checking readiness, reading application emails, and drafting replies. Prompts is the one place to edit them; every other page shows a short summary with Open in Prompts. You can rewrite a task's instructions, keep each version, and go back to any version or to the system default; every version is checked against our guardrails before it's saved. Every task runs on the one model of your AI setting. Its safety rules stay locked: text from a job page is only data, the answer is only data, and your deputy never browses, sends, applies, or pays because a page asks.

Open Score fit in Prompts

How is a new prompt version tested, and when does it roll back?

Before a version you write goes live, your deputy tests it: it replays the version on your last 40 decisions, scores how often it agrees with what you decided (its Test score), and runs the guardrail checks. You can leave while it runs; your deputy tells you when it's done. It fails if its Test score is below 80%, or more than 5 points below the Test score of the version in use. A version that fails never goes live: it reads Did not pass, with the reason, and the version in use stays.

A version that passes is tried on 20% of runs for 3 days first. Then Kept by you (of what it produced in real use, the share you kept) decides: below 80%, or more than 5 points below the version in use, and your deputy rolls it back by itself. Only a version on trial or in use is ever rolled back, by these rules or by you. Change these limits for your account or one prompt in Settings, Prompts.

How do AI credits work, and what do they cost?

AI credits pay for Deputy Assist on our platform. They're prepaid value, shown as tokens on your account's model. Every month brings free credits worth 250,000 tokens on Ministral 3 14B, refreshed on the 1st (UTC); a friend's first search earns you both bonus credits; and you can buy more. Free credits are used first, then bonus, then bought ones.

Credits cost what Amazon Bedrock charges for your model in your home Region, plus a 10% margin. For Ministral 3 14B in US East (N. Virginia), AWS charges $0.20 a million tokens, so its credits cost $0.22. Tax is added at checkout, based on your billing address. Your own key never uses credits.

Privacy & your data

Where is my data stored?

In the home Region you choose at sign-up: the United States, India, or the United Kingdom. Your profile, documents, searches, found roles, and encrypted keys stay there, and nothing is copied to another Region. When a run uses your own AI key, the job data it needs goes to that provider. This account's Region is the United States.

Can I change my home Region?

Not yet. The Region is set at sign-up and locked so your data never moves. Moving to another Region will mean exporting your data, deleting the account, and signing up there.

How do I delete my account or export my data?

Deleting your account erases your sign-in, profile, documents, searches, keys, site passwords, and every record about you in your home Region, except the agreements you signed: we keep those permanently, apart from your account, and a new account signs the agreement again. Export, a download of everything, is planned for launch. Request deletion in Settings, Account; you type delete my account to confirm. In this prototype nothing is deleted.

What does your deputy read in my email?

Only mail about your job search, once you connect Gmail, and read-only, so it can't send, delete, move, or change any message.

  • It reads
    • One-time sign-in codes from job sites
    • Emails from employers about your applications
  • It never
    • Reads other email, such as personal messages or newsletters
    • Sends email: you send every reply yourself
    • Keeps a one-time code: it’s shown once, then deleted

When an employer's email says your application moved forward or closed, your deputy moves it in Applications and tells you, and you can undo the move. When an email needs an answer, it can draft a reply for you to edit and send from your own email. It keeps only the facts it needs, such as the employer, the new status, and the subject line.

While Gmail is connected, your deputy scans it once a day, at 07:00 your time, over the last 48 hours of mail, and Scan now reads what came since. Each scan uses one run. Settings, Connections lists the last scans; in Applications, Sync from Gmail scans and moves the cards, and each card an email moved says so, with Undo.

Connected as alex.morgan@example.com since Sep 28; you can disconnect it any time in Settings, Connections. Gmail isn’t connected; you can connect it in Settings, Connections, after a consent step. Nothing reads email in this prototype.

Can my deputy create accounts on employers' sites, and where are the passwords?

Yes, when a site asks for an account before you can apply, in an application you started. Your deputy creates it in your name, with your email address, and you choose the password: a strong one your deputy generates (20 random characters), or your own (at least 12 characters, and not a common one). The site's one-time code is read from your Gmail, shown once, and never stored.

Passwords are kept encrypted in your vault, in your home Region, and used only to sign in to that site for your applications. You can see any of them in Settings, Connections after you confirm it's you: each stays hidden until you choose Show, and the vault log records every time one is shown. At a CAPTCHA your deputy stops and asks you; it never gets around one. Your site accounts are in Settings, Connections.

Why does Profile ask for self-identification?

Only so your deputy can answer employers' voluntary questions the way you choose. It's never used to rank or filter roles, never required, and never counted as missing. Every question offers Prefer not to say or I don’t wish to answer, and until you answer, your deputy chooses Decline to answer on forms. You can change or clear it any time.

Accessibility & motion

How do I stop the animations?

On the sign-in pages, Pause story holds the story. Once you're signed in, Pause motion in your deputy's panel (under Activity on smaller screens) stops every animation on every page until you press Play motion, and the sign-in story then waits for Play story. If your device asks for reduced motion, nothing moves at all.

Can I use JobDeputy with a keyboard, a screen reader, or zoom?

Yes. Every task works from the keyboard with visible focus, fields keep their labels, and changes are announced once. Pages reflow without sideways scrolling down to 320 pixels wide, about 400% zoom on a laptop, and a colour never carries meaning alone: each has a name and a count beside it.

Signing in

What if I forget my password?

Choose Forgot password? on Sign in and enter your email and your account's home Region. If they match an account, reset instructions arrive by email, and the link works once, for a short time.

Reset your password

Why can't I sign in?

Check the home Region first: accounts are looked up only in the Region you choose, so pick the one you signed up in, even when you travel. If you created the account with Google or LinkedIn, use Continue with Google or Continue with LinkedIn. For your privacy, the message never says whether an email has an account.

Can I sign in with LinkedIn, or import my details from it?

Yes. Continue with LinkedIn, on Sign in and Create account, signs you in: LinkedIn shares your name, email address, and profile photo with us, and nothing else. We never see your LinkedIn password or post anything for you.

To import your details, download your data from LinkedIn yourself (the ZIP file from Settings, Data privacy, Get a copy of your data) or save your profile as a PDF, then choose Import from LinkedIn in Settings, Connections. Your deputy shows each detail it found next to what your profile has and saves only the ones you choose; the file isn't kept. Import from LinkedIn is in Connections.
