# JobDeputy prototype map

**Status:** interaction prototype, round 7 of the signed-in redesign (the owner's sixth review: one flow, one home per thing, runs and tokens, and boot without flicker) under owner review · **Version date:** 2026-10-07

This map defines the connected browser prototype. New UI work extends these routes and
responsibilities instead of creating disconnected alternatives.

## Primary journey

1. **First time:** a person chooses the home Region first (round 6), then creates an account by
   email, with Google, or with LinkedIn (the simulated choosers name the Region), signs the User
   Agreement (in one step since round 7: one sentence and Sign and continue), and goes to setup.
   **Returning:** a person signs in by email, with Google, with LinkedIn, or with one tap and goes
   straight to the Dashboard, without setup and without replaying the opening loader; a returning
   account in another home Region is stopped with a calm notice before anything happens. Choosing a
   new password happens only after the separate recovery-link state.
2. Setup asks one question at a time, then opens the Dashboard with the first search running
   (`home-preview.html?skipLoader&state=new&from=onboarding`), where the first-run tour explains
   each tile and colour. Skip setup for now and Skip the rest (round 6) open the first run at any
   step and put each step left unanswered on the To-do. Every page's tour, given by the deputy from
   the tour card, can be replayed from Show me around (the account menu and the deputy), Take the
   tour, and Help's Demos. Help's Run setup again re-runs setup with the saved answers. The loader
   plays once, at the initial bootstrap.
3. The Dashboard shows the whole search at a glance, each figure once; every tile, segment, and key
   item opens its page and filter, which shows the same name, colour, and count. One sample world in
   `brand/sample-store.js` feeds every number: a search finishing on any page (the live Amazon
   search on a fresh browser) moves every count together, and every search uses one run: Get more
   runs (a pack in Purchases) moves the runs' "lasts through" date everywhere. One AI setting runs
   every search and draft, and a token limit on your own key pauses AI work or falls back to free
   platform runs when reached. A link into a source lands on its run in Searches without changing a
   filter. Help's See a large account shows every page with a much larger account. **Round 7
   (R10-2, R10-9):** the Dashboard is one glance per area, each screen opens with its own metrics
   band, and everything is runs or tokens: each search you start, each source a Keep watch check
   reads, each Gmail scan (daily at 07:00, or Scan now), and each daily email use one run, and the
   runway counts everything you turned on.
4. Searches find roles, Job matches is where the person decides, and Applications tracks only what
   they chose (the flow ribbon on all three pages shows this). Add to Applications moves a role into
   Applications as Shortlisted. **Round 7 (J2):** Job matches is a queue: adding a role or setting
   it aside takes it out and opens the next, and a role already in Applications opens there.
5. Tailoring shows a bounded pending state. Review actions stay hidden until the draft is ready, then
   open the materials editor with the selected application.
6. **Review, then auto-apply (round 6):** your deputy reviews its drafts, and the role waits in
   Applications' Ready for you with its review score and checks. One Approve and apply (there, at
   the top of the role panel, or at the top of Materials) approves the materials and starts the
   background job: the role's card shows each step and the time counting up, Watch it opens the
   autofill preview live, the agreement comes first where it applies, and any question the form
   asks waits on the card (Needs you) until answered. In the prototype nothing is sent and the
   role becomes Applied by your deputy. A person can still mark a role applied after applying on
   the employer's site, and How your deputy applies sets the rules. **Round 7 (A1, R10-4):** one
   board in flow order replaces Ready for you: your deputy tailors the roles you added each morning
   at 06:00 (or at once with Tailor now), the Approval card holds the review and Approve and apply,
   each card says who did each step, cards glide as things happen, and Sync from Gmail moves the
   cards its emails moved.
7. Feedback is one tap away on every signed-in page (the header, the account menu, Help, and "Was
   this match right?"), and Invite friends shows how bonus credits would be earned. Settings holds
   the account, the connections, the prompts (tested before any run uses a new version), AI &
   credits, and Purchases; since round 7 also Usage (what your runs and tokens go to) and Activity
   (everything you, your deputy, and JobDeputy did).
8. Nothing in the prototype applies for a role, contacts an employer, sends an email, calls Google,
   or claims that a disconnected API operation succeeded.

- **A link to something missing says so** (round 6, part 3, R9-2): a `?run=`, `?job=`,
  `?source=`, `?prompt=`, or `?stage=` that names nothing shows one calm notice in the page's own

- **Live parts never replace controls** (round 6, part 3, R9-3): a live run, a background apply, a
  running test, or a counting clock changes text and attributes in place, so a click or the focus
  is never lost to a redraw; where a control is drawn again, focus returns to it by its

- **One home per thing** (round 7, R10-3): a fact is edited where it lives; elsewhere a read-only

  \<page> (`data-page-back`), and coming back restores the scroll, the open panels, the filters, the
  chosen item, and focus. `return=` names only a page of this prototype in the same folder.
- **Boot without flicker** (round 7, R10-1): a page paints once, after its first render (the

  every page, usual and large, keeps layout shift at most 0.02 and no task over 200 ms.
- **The state is in the address** (round 7, R10-6 and R10-7): a list's page and page size, its

persistence, authorization, and tests exist.

## Completion gate

A prototype change is complete only when:

- every visible navigation action resolves to a page, anchor, external destination, download, or
  clearly scoped local interaction;
- loading controls expose pending text and disable duplicate submission;
- content that depends on a completed operation remains hidden or disabled beforehand;
- desktop, the `89px` rail, and exact `390px` and `320px` mobile layouts have no horizontal
  overflow;
- inline scripts parse, Biome has no errors, repository verification passes, and the approved
  animated identity remains unchanged.
